Chakra

Screen for real-world skill, at scale

Chakra is an agentic interviewer that spins up a canvas with a real-world problem, observes how candidates work and evaluates judgment, critical thinking & AI fluency.

Chakra spins up a live canvas with a realistic problem and watches how candidates plan, build and debug — measuring judgment, critical thinking and AI fluency, not whether they memorized an algorithm.

From frontend and backend to data, ML and DevOps, spin up real-world interviews tailored to the exact role you are hiring for.

Give every applicant a fair, consistent first round without scheduling or manual review.

Integrity isn’t about whether candidates use AI — it’s about following the rules. Chakra flags suspicious behavior like unauthorized tools, external devices or outside help.

Every score maps to a clear rubric and links back to the evidence behind it.

Interview

Interview on the work that matters

Interview is a pair-programming product where the interviewer and candidate work on the same canvas on a real-world task — a glimpse of what it is like to actually work together.

Interviewer and candidate build on one shared canvas in real time, on a task that mirrors the actual job.

Everything needed to collaborate is built in — no juggling extra tools during the session.

Swap trick questions for the work your team actually does and see how someone really performs.

Capture notes and scores against a consistent rubric so decisions stay comparable and defensible.
